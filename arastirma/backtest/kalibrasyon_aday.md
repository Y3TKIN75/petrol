Observed Brent reactions, 31 Aug - 17 Sep 2026 (wartime regime: tight physical market, daily ranges of 3-6%):
- Major export infrastructure physically damaged or shut (pipeline, loading terminal), confirmed: +3% to +8%, lasting days. Example: Saudi Hormuz-bypass pipeline shut by drones -> +3.5% at the next open; loadings suspended and cargoes cancelled -> +3% futures, +8% physical.
- Credible official signal that a disruption will be short or repaired soon (energy secretary, producer, company): -2% to -4% within a day, reversing part of the earlier spike. Restoration/rerouting news moves prices about as much as the disruption did; do not treat it as minor.
- Rerouting exports through alternative ports/routes: -1% to -3%.
- Attacks on tankers or producer energy facilities, or escalation spreading to a new producer/region: +3% to +6%.
- Renewed direct military exchange after a calm period: +1% to +3%.
- Leader statements that rule out a deal or promise escalation coincided with large up-days (+3% or more) when the market was already tense.
- Demand/macro news (agency demand forecasts, central-bank decisions): typically below 1.5%.
In this regime a confirmed supply event of the kinds above is usually "orta" or "yuksek", rarely "dusuk".
